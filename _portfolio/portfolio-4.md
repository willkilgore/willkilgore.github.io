## Objectives
Increase manufacturability and decrease post-processing time for ready-for-sale carbon fiber bike parts

## Overview
At my internship with Parlee Cycles, I developed a new layup schedule that cut production speed of derailleur mounts by 30%. The updated layup  reduced void content in the cured parts, significantly decreasing post-processing effort while maintaining structural performance. Parts were produced out of prepreg carbon fiber using this new layup process and baked while pressure was applied. After curing, parts were finished and packaged for retail sale.

## Technical Details
- Layup patterns designed in Patternsmith then cut out with an Autometrix plotter
- Varying weights of preimpregnated carbon fiber used to balance stiffness and weight requirements
- Optimzed heat and pressure applied during curing to reduce void content and quicken production cycle
- Mill and other shop tools used for part separation and additional machining
- Final parts were sanded and wax-coated to improve UV and moisture resistance
- Ready-for-sale bags assembled with included spacers and stainless steel hardware
- Process was adopted for regular production use moving forward
