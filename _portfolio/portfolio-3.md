## Objectives
Optimize the geometry of an acrylic structural bracket to maximize strength-to-weight and stiffness-to-weight ratios through finite element analysis

## Overview
This was a group project completed as part of a class on deformable solids, in which I was responsible for the strenght-to-weight optimization of an acrylic bracket. This project started by considering the geometry restriction placed on us, then required proper setting up of a model using the student edition of Abaqus. After boundary conditions and loading were applied on the part, the geometry was iteratively refined using stress and displacement results from the FEA.

## Technical Details
- Geometrical restrictions assessed to create maximum part envelope
- Mesh was created on part with local seeding in high-stress regions
- Boundary conditions and loading cases were applied to represent real world conditions
- Design was iterated to minimize weight and maximize strength and weight of the final product
- Final geometry was laser-cut out of acrylic and then tested to failure to verify predicted values
