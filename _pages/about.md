I am a Mechanical Engineering student at McGill University with experience in rapid prototyping, composite manufacturing, electromechanical systems, and product development. I have hands-on experience designing tooling and fixtures, creating technical drawings, conducting vibration analysis, and supporting prototype robotic and bicycle systems from design through manufacturing. I am interested in mechanical design, manufacturing engineering, and building high-performance physical products through iterative engineering and testing.

I currently apply these skills as a co-lead of McGill Rocket Team's Airframe Composites project, where I coordinate layups and aerostructures integration of a 10-member subteam. I am also the V.P. of Finance for the McGill Cycling Team, where I am responsible for managing money from fundraising, sponsorships, and team fees. Outside of engineering, I enjoy photography, cooking, and spending time outdoors!
